# Landing page

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Filipino small-store owners with a menu or catalog. Job: show that PasaOrder joins QR/link ordering and owner-reviewed Messenger order drafts in one order workspace. Primary action: create an account at /register. Proof: real product capabilities, two generated editorial images, and an explicitly illustrative chat-to-draft example. No testimonials, invented metrics, pricing, automatic payment confirmation, or Instagram claim.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Orders move from customer touchpoint to owner action. Refuse the generic AI SaaS hero and equal feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Existing PasaOrder forest green, soft lime, warm neutral, Geist, plain rounded controls. Editorial shop photography gives the page a human center; clean operational typography and precise rules carry the rest.

STORY: Recognize a local owner handling many channels; understand that one catalog and one order queue connect QR/link checkout with reviewed Messenger drafts; create an account.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Warm cream, large left-aligned headline, concise right-hand copy and create-account action, then a wide merchant photograph. A forest handoff strip connects menu or Messenger to owner review and one order queue. On phones the copy and handoff stack; sign-in remains in the sticky header.

FORM: Handoff sequence, fourth in the grounded structural list; surface seed f04dc3f6. Signature moment: a visitor-controlled conversation becomes an editable draft and then a confirmed queue entry. Motion is finite and user-initiated, paused offscreen, stopped on hidden tabs, with manual reduced-motion alternatives. Supporting reveals run once with visible defaults. Existing photography remains in use.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
